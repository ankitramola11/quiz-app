📋 Rules & Guidelines

  • ⏱️ The timer starts as soon as you click Start Test and cannot be paused.
  • 🔄 You can navigate freely between questions using the Previous/Next buttons or the question palette.
  • 💾 Your answers are auto-saved after each selection. Refreshing the page will not lose your progress.
  • 🚨 When time expires, the test is automatically submitted — even if you haven't submitted manually.
  • 🔒 Each student gets only 1 attempt. You cannot re-attempt the quiz after submission.
  • 📵 Do NOT switch tabs or windows during the quiz. Your test will be auto-submitted immediately.
  • 📡 Ensure a stable internet connection throughout the test.
  • ❌ Skipped/unattempted questions carry zero marks. No negative marking.
  • 🖥️ Use a laptop or desktop for the best experience. Mobile is supported but not recommended.
